About

BC Buddy gives every Business Central environment its own colour: ribbon, frame, banner, tab title and favicon. One glance tells you whether you are in production, a sandbox or a demo, so you never change the wrong environment by accident.

It also makes the web client easier to work in: pages open in wide layout, tile pages switch to list view, dropdowns get more space and the line you are on stands out.
