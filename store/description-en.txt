Business Central, easier to work in.

Copy any value in one click, see every page full width, and always know which environment you are in, right in the Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central web client. BC Buddy takes the small frustrations out of your working day.

Free. Works the moment you install it.

⚡ Get through your work faster

No more clicking the same buttons on every page.

• One-click copy: any amount, No. or link, even the ones you could never select
• Every page full width, with the action bar pinned
• Lists instead of tiles
• Dropdowns that show all their columns: no more scrolling sideways
• Help, Copilot and Page Inspection as wide as you drag them
• Week numbers in the date picker
• The line you are on stands out in journals and orders
• No notifications bell in your way

Your choices win: set a page back to narrow, and BC Buddy leaves it that way.

Want more? Switch on striped rows, a panel with the keyboard shortcuts for the page you are on, or turn off animations. Every improvement has its own switch.

🎨 Never post in the wrong environment

Production and sandbox look exactly alike. With BC Buddy, they don't.

• The top bar in your colour, with your text: "PRODUCTION - CRONUS BE"
• A coloured frame around the window
• A banner, or a ribbon in the corner
• A marked tab title and a coloured tab icon, easy to spot among ten tabs

Choose a colour per environment, per company or per customer. Or start from ready-made samples in one click.

👥 Set it up once for the whole team

One person picks the colours, everyone else gets them automatically, later changes included.

🔒 Your data stays yours

No account. No server. No tracking. BC Buddy changes how Business Central looks, never your records.
