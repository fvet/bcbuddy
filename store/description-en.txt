BC Buddy makes the Business Central web client easier to work in, and gives every environment its own colour so you never post in production by mistake.

An easier web client, straight away. No setup needed:

- Copy any value with one click, also an amount or a No. you cannot select.
- Every page in wide layout with its action bar pinned, and lists instead of tiles.
- Dropdowns that show all their columns, so you no longer scroll sideways.
- Week numbers in the calendar of a date field.
- A keyboard icon that shows the shortcuts for the page and field you are in.
- The line you are on in a journal or order stands out.
- If you like, striped rows, and narrower columns so more of a list or journal fits on the screen.
- No notifications bell, and if you like, no animations.

Each one has its own switch in the options.

A colour for every environment. A test environment and the real one look exactly alike. BC Buddy marks them, so you see at a glance where you are:

- The bar at the top of Business Central takes your colour and your text, for example "PRODUCTION - CRONUS BE".
- A coloured frame around the window.
- A banner along the bottom, or a ribbon in the corner.
- The tab title, for example "[TEST] Company Information".
- A coloured icon on the tab, so you find the right one among ten others.

You decide when each colour appears: per environment, per company or per customer. Production green, sandbox red - or whatever suits you.

Working in a team? One person sets up the colours and everyone else gets them automatically, including later changes.

Works with Business Central online and on your own server. Available in English, Dutch and French.

Everything stays on your own computer: no account, no server, no tracking.
