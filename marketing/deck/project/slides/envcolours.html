See at a glance where you are

Without BC Buddy

Test and production look exactly alike. Nothing says where you are.

With BC Buddy

Production in green: its name in the top bar,
a frame and a banner in the corner.

Without BC Buddy every environment shows the same top bar, so a test environment and the real one look exactly alike. One wrong tab and you post for real. BC Buddy colours the top bar per environment, with your own text, and can add a frame, a banner, a marking in the tab title and a coloured tab icon. You choose the colour per environment, per company or per customer. One person sets the colours up and shares them with the team in one file; everyone gets later changes automatically.
