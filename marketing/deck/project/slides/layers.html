Business Central has three layers. At the bottom, the database: in the cloud Microsoft runs it and nobody changes it directly. In the middle, the business logic in AL: posting, validation, pages and reports. That is where partners and AppSource apps customise BC with extensions, which means development, testing and re-testing at every BC update. On top, everything that uses BC: the web client, the tablet and phone apps, Teams, Copilot and agents, MCP, APIs and SOAP. BC Buddy only touches one of those: the web client, inside each person's own browser. It adds no code to BC and has no effect on the other layers or on other users.
