The first question is usually: does it touch our ERP? No. BC Buddy runs in the browser, on top of the page Business Central already shows. There is no extension to install in BC, no partner involvement and no upgrade risk. It has no account and no server, and it sends nothing anywhere. Each person can switch every improvement on or off, and one switch pauses all of it. It is free, comes from the Chrome Web Store, and works in Chrome and Edge, for BC online and on-premises.
