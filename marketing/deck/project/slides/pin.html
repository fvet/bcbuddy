Pin the action bar

Actions always in reach

Without BC Buddy

The actions hide
behind the tabs:
one more click, every time.

With BC Buddy

The action bar is pinned:
Post, Release and more
stay in view.

Under the tabs of a page sits a row of actions such as Post and Release. Business Central often hides that row: you click a tab first, and only then see the actions. BC Buddy pins the action bar the first time a page opens, so the actions stay in view. Business Central remembers a page you pinned or unpinned yourself, and BC Buddy leaves that choice alone.
