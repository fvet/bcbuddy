Without BC Buddy

The card opens as a narrow window over the list.
Labels get cut off.

With BC Buddy

Every page opens in wide layout:
more room for fields, nothing cut off.

By default Business Central opens a card as a narrow window over the list you came from. Long labels such as Balance (LCY) As Vendor get cut off and you see fewer fields. BC Buddy opens every page in wide layout, and tile pages as a list. If someone switches a page back to narrow, BC Buddy respects that choice.
