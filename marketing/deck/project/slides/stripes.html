Striped rows

Read across a wide list without slipping a row

Without BC Buddy

Every row is white. Halfway across, your eye slips to the next one.

With BC Buddy

Every other row a light grey, from the first column to the last.

In a wide list such as General Ledger Entries, every row is white, and by the time you read the amount at the far right it is easy to land on the row below. BC Buddy gives every other row a light grey, so your eye stays on the right line. The line you are on and the lines you select keep Business Central's own colours. The grey is deliberately light, so it is easier to see on a screen than on a projector. This one starts off: each person switches it on in the settings.
