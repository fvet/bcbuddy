Keyboard shortcuts

The shortcuts for where you are, one click away

Business Central hides its shortcuts at the end of a button's tooltip. BC Buddy puts a keyboard icon in the top bar that opens them in a side panel:

On this page: the page's own actions, such as Post (F9)

Where your cursor is: the lines, the list, the card or the date field

Anywhere: a few that always work, such as Alt+Q

The side panel sits next to Business Central, not on top of it, and changes along with the page.

Keyboard shortcuts save a lot of clicks, but Business Central keeps them out of sight: the only place it shows one is at the end of a button's tooltip, and the full list is a long article on Microsoft's site. BC Buddy adds a keyboard icon to the top bar. One click opens a side panel next to Business Central with the shortcuts that work where you are: the page's own actions, such as Post with F9, what works in the lines, the list or the date field you are in, and a few that work anywhere. The panel stays open, changes along when you open another page, and closes by itself when you leave Business Central. This one starts off: each person switches it on in the settings.
