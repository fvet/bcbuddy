Without BC Buddy

Planning talks in weeks, but BC's calendar doesn't show them.

With BC Buddy

The week number in front of each week, counted the way BC counts weeks.

Delivery dates, production and holidays are planned in weeks, but the calendar of a date field in Business Central doesn't show week numbers. BC Buddy adds them in front of each week. They are counted the way Business Central counts weeks everywhere else: weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the first Thursday of the year.
