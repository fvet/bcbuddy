Help & feedback

Report a problem without leaving Business Central

The BC Buddy icon in the top bar opens Report a problem in a side panel next to Business Central:

Step by step: a line of text and a screenshot of each step, the whole screen or just a region

Point it out: an arrow, a pen or a box, and blur what others must not see

Email: the report opens in your own email app, with the company, the environment and a link to each page

IT sets the helpdesk address and the priorities once, for everyone.

When something goes wrong in Business Central, the helpdesk usually gets a vague email and has to ask which company, which page and what exactly happened. With Help and feedback, people report it straight from Business Central. The BC Buddy icon in the top bar opens a side panel next to the page. They write what they did one step at a time and take a screenshot of each step, of the whole screen or just the part that matters. On a screenshot they can draw an arrow, a pen line or a box, and blur names, amounts or anything else that is private. One click on Email opens the report in their own email app, with the screenshots inside, the environment and company, and a link to each page. Nothing goes through a server of ours. It starts off: IT switches it on and sets the helpdesk address, the priorities and the email subjects for everyone, through the team file or by policy.
