Never lose your place on a line

Without BC Buddy

Only a small arrow at the far left
shows which line you are on.

With BC Buddy

The whole line gets a light tint,
edge to edge.

On the lines of a sales order or in a journal, Business Central marks the line you are on with only a small arrow at the far left. On a wide line with many columns it is easy to lose your place. BC Buddy gives the whole line a light tint, from the first column to the last. The tint is deliberately soft so it doesn't distract while you type; the orange frame on this slide only points it out.
