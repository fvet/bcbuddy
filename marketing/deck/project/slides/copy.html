Any value, copied in one click

Without BC Buddy

A No. or an amount is a link: clicking it opens a page instead of selecting it.

With BC Buddy

Point at a value,
click the copy icon,
paste it anywhere.

Copying a customer number or an amount out of Business Central is surprisingly hard: most values in a list are links, so clicking one opens a page instead of selecting it. With BC Buddy, resting the mouse on a value shows a small copy icon, circled here next to customer 20000. One click copies the value exactly as BC shows it, ready to paste into Excel, an email or Teams. It works in lists, in FactBoxes and on link fields on a card.
