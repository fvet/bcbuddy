Dropdown size

Dropdowns that show every column

Without BC Buddy

Only No. and Description fit. The rest hides behind a sideways scrollbar.

With BC Buddy

As wide as its columns need: unit, price and inventory at a glance.

When you pick an item on a sales line, Business Central shows a small dropdown with room for two columns. Unit, price and inventory are there, but you have to scroll sideways to see them. BC Buddy makes the dropdown as wide as its columns need, so you choose the right item at once. Width and height each have a slider in the settings.
